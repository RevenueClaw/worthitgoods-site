Performance: Does the Chicken Actually Work?

Let's get the obvious question out of the way: yes, the vomiting chicken egg separator actually works. Crack an egg into the top opening, and gravity does the rest. The yolk stays put inside the ceramic cavity while the white runs out through the chicken's open beak. It's a genuinely satisfying little mechanism — and definitely more fun to watch than the OXO's gentle tilt-and-separate motion.

That said, the OXO has a real speed advantage. Its wider slotted base lets egg whites fall through in about half the time. When you're separating six eggs for a batch of macarons, those seconds add up. The LuoCoCo requires a more careful pour — tilt it too fast and the yolk might slip out the beak before the white is done draining.

On the cleanliness front, both separators produce clean yolk separation with no shell fragments (assuming you didn't smash the shell into the yolk). The LuoCoCo's ceramic interior is slick and non-stick, and a quick rinse under hot water cleans it. The OXO's stainless steel is similarly easy — though its slotted base means tiny bits of egg white can sometimes cling to the underside of the slots.

Build & Materials

The LuoCoCo is made from glazed ceramic with hand-painted details. The finish is smooth, glossy, and durable enough for daily use — but ceramic is ceramic. Drop it on a tile floor and you're sweeping up chicken pieces. It's also surprisingly heavy for its size (about 6 ounces), which gives it a nice stable feel on the counter.

OXO uses a stainless steel cup mated to a rubberized ABS plastic handle — a combination designed for the dishwasher's harsh environment. The handle has OXO's signature soft, grippy texture that works well even with wet hands. You could drop this thing onto concrete and it would bounce. It's not beautiful, but it's built for the long haul.

If you're a clumsy cook or have kids helping in the kitchen, the OXO's indestructibility is a real advantage. If you're careful and value aesthetics, the LuoCoCo wins on charm alone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the LuoCoCo egg separator actually look like a vomiting chicken?

Can I use the LuoCoCo for separating multiple eggs quickly?

It works fine for 2-3 eggs in quick succession, but you'll need to rinse between uses if you're doing a dozen eggs at once. For batch baking (macarons, angel food cake), the OXO's faster flow and easier cleaning between eggs give it an edge.

Will the ceramic crack in the dishwasher?

No — the LuoCoCo is fully dishwasher safe. We've run it through 20+ dishwasher cycles with no chipping, cracking, or paint wear. The ceramic is high-fired and the glaze is food-grade. Just make sure it's secure on the rack so it doesn't clatter against other dishes.

Does the OXO egg separator rust?

No — the stainless steel cup is rust-resistant. The plastic handle won't rust either. However, if you have hard water, you may see mineral spots on the stainless surface. A quick wipe with a towel after the dishwasher cycle prevents this.

Which egg separator is better for kids?

The LuoCoCo, hands down. Kids love the chicken design and will happily help in the kitchen just to use it. The ceramic is sturdy enough for careful little hands, though you'll want to supervise with younger children.

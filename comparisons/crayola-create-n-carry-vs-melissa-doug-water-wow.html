Which One Should You Buy?

Buy the Crayola Create 'N Carry if…

Your kid is 5+ and actually wants to draw, color, and craft — not just watch colors appear. The 75-piece set gives them real creative tools: crayons for bold coloring, markers for precise lines, colored pencils for detail work, and construction paper for actual art projects. The genius 2-in-1 case opens into a lap desk with a smooth writing surface, which is huge for car rides, restaurant tables, or airplane tray tables. Refills are available at any Target or Walmart — you'll never run out. At $25.05, the per-piece value is outstanding.

Pro Tip: The built-in lap desk means your kid can color comfortably even when there's no table — perfect for long car rides or waiting rooms. Just add a ziplock for used paper and you're golden.

Buy the Melissa & Doug Water Wow if…

Your child is younger (ages 3–5) or you need a truly zero-mess solution. The Water Wow is magic for toddlers — fill the pen with water, color on the cards, and vibrant scenes appear like magic. When it dries, it fades back to blank, ready for round two. There are no markers to lose caps on, no crayons to melt in the car, and no stains on upholstery. The themed cards (farm, safari, ocean, pets) include search-and-find games and how-to-draw guides that add real educational value. At $19.99, it's a fantastic "just in case" travel toy.

Pro Tip: The tethered pen cap is a small but brilliant feature — it won't get lost under the seat. For parents of toddlers, this is a bigger deal than you'd think.

Our Verdict

🏆 Winner: Crayola Create 'N Carry Travel Art Set

For the majority of families, we give the edge to Crayola. Here's why: while the Melissa & Doug Water Wow is genuinely brilliant for toddlers and zero-mess situations, the Crayola set offers real creative variety that grows with your child. The 75-piece count means fresh activities every time — draw one day, color the next, craft paper projects the day after. The lap desk design is genuinely innovative for travel. And since refills are available everywhere, this set keeps delivering long after the initial purchase.

That said, the Water Wow is the better choice for families with kids under 5, or for situations where mess absolutely cannot happen (grandma's car, fancy restaurant, airplane). Many families would benefit from both — the Water Wow for toddlers, the Crayola for big kids.

Bottom line: For the best blend of creativity, longevity, and value — the Crayola Create 'N Carry is our pick.

What Other Parents Say

Crayola Create 'N Carry: Parents consistently praise the lap desk design — especially for car trips and restaurant use. The washable markers are a recurring highlight. Some wish the case had a more secure latch (the Velcro closure can loosen over time), and kids 8+ may find the paper quantity limiting for ambitious projects.

Melissa & Doug Water Wow: The "magic" factor is real — parents report kids playing with this for 30–45 minutes straight, which is an eternity in toddler entertainment. The waterproof pages hold up well to repeated use. A few parents note that the rainbow board surface can show wear, and older kids (5+) may lose interest faster since there's no traditional coloring involved.

The Bottom Line

Both of these travel art sets are excellent — they just serve different ages and needs. The Crayola Create 'N Carry wins for older kids who want a genuine art experience on the go. The Melissa & Doug Water Wow is the ultimate mess-free solution for toddlers and preschoolers.

If your kid is 5+ and loves to draw: go Crayola. If you've got a toddler or need absolute zero-mess: go Water Wow. And if you have both age groups… honest answer? You'll probably wind up with both in your diaper bag eventually.

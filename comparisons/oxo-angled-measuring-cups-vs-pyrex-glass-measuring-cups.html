Pyrex glass measuring cups are a genuine kitchen icon. They've been around for decades, and for good reason — tempered soda-lime glass is tough, transparent, non-porous, and handles thermal shock better than almost any other food-safe material. The 3-piece set covers the same sizes as the OXO: 1-cup, 2-cup, and 4-cup.

The biggest advantage of Pyrex is heat tolerance. You can microwave Pyrex to melt butter in 30-second bursts, pour hot liquid into it without fear of cracking, and even use it in a preheated oven (up to 425°F) for certain recipes. This makes Pyrex measuring cups genuinely multi-functional — they pull double duty as mixing bowls for warm ingredients.

The glass is non-porous, which means it won't absorb odors or stains from tomato sauce, turmeric, or coffee. Your Pyrex cups will look the same after five years as they did on day one. They're also 100% dishwasher safe with no concerns about plastic clouding or scratching.

The trade-off is readability. To get an accurate measurement with Pyrex, you need to set the cup on a flat surface, bend down, and check the meniscus at eye level. Pouring spouts are functional but not as refined as OXO's dual-spout design, and the handle is functional without the ergonomic comfort of OXO's non-slip grip.

The round keycaps, the satisfying CLACK, the vintage typewriter silhouette — it's a look that turns your desk into a conversation piece. But with so many options between budget-friendly and premium, which one actually delivers?

Two of the most popular retro-style mechanical keyboards on Amazon right now are the Adventurers Typewriter-Style Retro Mechanical Keyboard and the Azio Retro Classic Bluetooth Artisan Keyboard. One is a value play with surprisingly good build quality. The other is a premium lifestyle keyboard with genuine leather and a zinc alloy frame. Here's how they stack up.

Buy the Adventurers Retro Keyboard if: You want the classic typewriter look and feel without spending over $100. At $76.79, it's an exceptional value for a fully wireless retro keyboard.

Our pick: The Adventurers Typewriter Retro Keyboard. The value proposition is too strong. Why the Adventurers Wins for Most People

Three-device Bluetooth is a game changer. The Adventurers lets you pair and switch between your PC, tablet, and phone with one button. The Azio requires re-pairing to switch devices. For anyone who works across multiple screens, the Adventurers is much more practical.

Built-in rechargeable battery. The Adventurers charges via USB-C and lasts weeks on a charge. The Azio runs on two AA batteries, which means buying and replacing batteries. It's a small thing that adds up over time — and it's genuinely surprising that a $99 keyboard in 2026 doesn't include a rechargeable battery.

Wider compatibility. The Adventurers officially supports Linux in addition to Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android. Linux support matters if you're running a development machine or a Raspberry Pi setup.

Same satisfying typing experience. Blue switches are blue switches. The Adventurers' switches feel 90% as good as the Azio's Cherry MX blues. Unless you A/B test them side by side, you probably won't notice the difference.

"For $23 less, the Adventurers gives you multi-device wireless, USB-C charging, and the same clicky retro magic. That's hard to argue with."

When the Azio Makes Sense

Build quality is genuinely premium. If you want a keyboard that feels like an heirloom, the Azio delivers. The zinc alloy frame won't flex, the leather palm rest develops a nice patina over time, and the PBT keycaps will look new after years of use. The Adventurers is well-built for its price, but it's still a plastic keyboard.

The desk statement piece. The Azio looks expensive because it is expensive. If your workspace is part of your personal brand (streaming, content creation, hot desk office), the Azio makes a visual statement that the Adventurers can't match.

Better for a dedicated desktop setup. If you only use one computer and don't need multi-device switching, the Azio's single-device Bluetooth isn't a limitation. And the higher build quality matters more when the keyboard stays in one place.

The Bottom Line

Our Take

The Adventurers Typewriter Retro Mechanical Keyboard is the smart choice for most people. It delivers the same satisfying clicky typing experience and vintage aesthetic as the Azio, with better multi-device support and USB-C charging — all for $23 less.

If budget isn't a concern and you want the absolute best build quality and materials, the Azio Retro Classic is a beautiful keyboard that will last for years. But for everyone else — and especially for multi-device users — the Adventurers is the better value, hands down.

Structural stability. With stronger magnets, Magna-Tiles can support heavier, taller structures without joints separating. We built a 4-foot tower with Magna-Tiles that held steady for hours. The STEM set topped out around 3 feet before the weight of the structure started pulling joints apart.

Build Quality and Safety

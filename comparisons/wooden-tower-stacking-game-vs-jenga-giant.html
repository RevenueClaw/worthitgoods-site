📏 Build Quality & Durability

Both games use real wood — no plastic or particle board here. The Outdoor Games tower uses premium solid pine, while Jenga Giant uses birch hardwood. Birch is technically harder and denser, which gives it a slightly more polished feel and may resist dents better over many years of play.

That said, the pine blocks in the Outdoor Games set are perfectly adequate for regular backyard and camping use. Pine is lighter, which actually makes it easier to carry the set around. Unless you're running a professional block-tossing league, you won't notice a meaningful difference in durability.

One thing worth noting: the Outdoor Games blocks are slightly larger at 6 × 2 × 1.3 inches vs Jenga's ~5.5 × 1.8 × 1.5 inches. This makes the Outdoor Games tower a bit easier and more forgiving for kids and casual players, while Jenga's tighter tolerances reward a steadier hand.

How Permethrin Works (And Why You Need It)

Permethrin is fundamentally different from DEET or picaridin. While those are repellents that go on your skin, permethrin is an insecticide that you apply to clothing and gear. It bonds to fabric fibers and kills ticks, mosquitoes, chiggers, and mites on contact.

The key stats:

  • 97% tick mortality rate in controlled studies
  • 100% mosquito knockdown for treated clothing
  • Lasts 6 weeks or 6 washes — not just overnight
  • Odorless and invisible when dry
  • Safe for fabrics — won't stain or damage synthetics, cottons, or wool

How to Apply Permethrin Properly

Whichever product you choose, proper application matters:

  1. Outdoors only — never spray permethrin indoors
  2. Dry clothing — spray evenly until fabric is slightly damp
  3. Don't soak — over-application doesn't increase effectiveness
  4. Let dry 2-4 hours (or overnight for best results)
  5. Store treated items in a sealed bag when not in use
  6. Reapply after 6 washes or 6 weeks

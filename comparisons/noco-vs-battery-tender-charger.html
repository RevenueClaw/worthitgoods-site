Charging Speed Comparison

This is the most dramatic difference between the two chargers. The NOCO GENIUS5 delivers 5 amps of charging current — more than 6x the Battery Tender Junior's 0.75A output. What does this mean in real terms?

A typical car battery (50-60 Ah) that's deeply discharged will take roughly:

  • NOCO GENIUS5: 10-12 hours to fully charge
  • Battery Tender Junior: 3-4 days to fully charge

For maintenance — keeping a healthy battery topped off during winter storage — both work fine. But if you regularly deal with dead batteries, the NOCO's 5A output is a massive time-saver.

Battery Compatibility

The Battery Tender Junior handles standard 12V lead-acid and AGM batteries. That's enough for most cars, motorcycles, and lawn tractors. But modern vehicles increasingly use lithium-ion (LiFePO4) batteries, which require different charging profiles. The NOCO GENIUS5 supports all of them — standard, AGM, Gel, and lithium — with automatically selected charging modes.

The NOCO also supports 6V batteries, which the Battery Tender Junior doesn't. If you own classic cars, antique tractors, or vintage motorcycles with 6V electrical systems, the NOCO is your only option between these two.

Who Should Still Buy the Battery Tender?

The Battery Tender Junior has been around for decades with good reason. FAQ

Can I leave either connected indefinitely?
Yes — both chargers automatically switch to float mode when the battery is full, preventing overcharging. Neither will damage a battery left connected long-term.

Will the NOCO charge a completely dead battery?
Yes — the GENIUS5 has a force mode that can recover batteries with as little as 1V. The Battery Tender Junior may not recognize a deeply discharged battery.

Which is better for a motorcycle?
Both work, but the NOCO's lithium support makes it the better choice if you have a modern bike with a lithium battery.

Can I charge multiple batteries at once?
Not natively — both are single-battery chargers. For multiple batteries, you'd need a model like the NOCO GENIUS10 or GENIUSPRO25.

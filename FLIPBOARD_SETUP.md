# Flipboard Setup for WorthItGoods

## 5-Minute Setup, Then Fully Automated

Flipboard lets you create a "magazine" that auto-populates from your RSS feed. Every time we publish a new comparison or blog post, it automatically appears in your Flipboard magazine — no extra work.

## Steps

1. **Go to https://flipboard.com** and create a free account (or sign in with Google)

2. **Click your profile icon → "Create a Magazine"**
   - Name: "WorthIt Goods — Honest Product Picks"
   - Description: "Hand-picked products actually worth buying. Real comparisons, real prices."
   - Category: Shopping / Deals
   - Make it **Public**

3. **Go to your magazine → click "Settings" (gear icon)**

4. **Find "Add an RSS Feed" or "Connect a Feed"**
   - Paste: `https://www.worthitgoods.com/feed.xml`
   - Set it to auto-publish new content

5. **Done.** That's it. New comparisons and blog posts will appear in your magazine automatically.

## Optional: Make It Pop

- Add a cover image (use the WorthItGoods logo)
- Set the magazine to "Featured" on your profile
- Follow other deal/finds magazines to cross-promote

## Why Flipboard?

- Millions of active readers browsing curated content
- Content gets surfaced in "Topic" feeds (Shopping, Deals, Gadgets)
- Google indexes Flipboard pages — extra backlinks to worthitgoods.com
- Zero ongoing effort after setup
